
# Clout City: Who Killed Sarah Bird? (v2)

2026-10-11 · @Internovatechnologies

## Overview

**Logline:** Queens' brightest songbird dies after a Sunday "blessing brunch," and every person in her life had a reason to fear the announcement she was due to make the next day.

The arc runs 10 beats over 10 days: 8 investigation beats, a reveal on Day 9 and an aftermath on Day 10. The killer is Pastor Gideon Vale. The main feed points hard at the boyfriend, the side boyfriend and the mother, while planting fair-play clues for the pastor that free players can catch.

**Anchor:** Long Island City, Queens (Sarah's building, Skyline Pier Residences), with a second hot spot in Astoria (Radiant Harbor Church). Both are in the starting borough, so every free player can visit them.

**Narrator:** Lola Lowdown, "Queens' most reliable unreliable source." She is gossipy about the circus around the death (ring-light vigils, grief merch, comment-section detectives) and never jokes about Sarah herself. Every beat about Sarah ends soft; every beat about the suspects ends sharp.

**Tone:** This is the darkest arc type the Quality Brief allows. The death stays off-screen, the cause is only ever "a sedative in her tea," and nothing is graphic.

**Clock rules:** One story day equals one real day on the New York clock. The arc's start date must be a Sunday, so weekday references in the copy ("Sunday brunch," "Monday's livestream") are always correct. The story never uses calendar dates.

**Launch framing:** If this is the first story new players see after the tutorial, the home-screen prompt frames it as a mystery, not a death: "Queens has a whodunit. Think you can crack it?" The first beat card then delivers the news.

## Answer key (hidden until Day 9)

Pastor Gideon Vale killed Sarah Bird. He had quietly moved $1.2M out of the Bird Song Foundation into a shell company funding his Harbor Dome campus. On the Friday before she died, Sarah emailed her accountant asking for a full audit of the foundation. Her Monday livestream would announce that she was leaving Radiant Harbor, handing the foundation to independent trustees and firing her mother as manager.

**What happened on Sunday (off-screen):**

- 11:00 AM: Loretta and Vale arrive for Sunday "blessing brunch." Vale brings his signature chamomile tea in a Radiant Harbor sun-logo thermos, as he does at every visit.
- 12:10 PM: Loretta and Sarah argue about the management contract. Loretta storms out.
- 12:10 to 12:52 PM: Vale stays alone with Sarah. He pours her a cup from the thermos, which he has laced with a sedative. Sarah posts her last story at 12:31: the teacup, captioned "one last cup before the big news."
- 12:52 PM: Vale signs out and takes the thermos with him. He later tells reporters he left "right behind Loretta."
- 1:34 PM: Jett's fob opens the garage. He collects sneaker boxes from his car and never goes upstairs (elevator logs, revealed Day 9).
- 2:15 PM: Theo enters through the service door, knocks, gets no answer, and leaves white roses and a note.
- 3:50 PM: Sarah's stylist can't reach her for a fitting. Building staff open the door. She is pronounced dead at 4:30 PM.

**Where the thermos is:** washed and back on the shelf in Radiant Harbor's hospitality room, logo and all.

**What the others really did:** Jett stole $40,000 but did not kill her. Theo came to apologize. Loretta's fight was about money she believed she was owed, and her sleeping pills are a different drug from the one found in the tea.

## Main arc beats

Each beat is a headline plus 2 to 3 sentences, with a one-line "Previously" for latecomers. Voice: Lola Lowdown.

### Beat 1 (Day 1, Sunday): "Queens' Glass Bird Goes Quiet"

*Previously:* nothing. This is where it starts.

Sarah Bird, 27, the voice behind "Glass Bird" and 7.5 million followers, was found unconscious in her Long Island City penthouse and pronounced dead this afternoon at 4:30 PM. She'd spent Sunday brunch with her mother, Loretta, and Pastor Gideon Vale, who brought his famous chamomile blessing tea, as always. Everyone is cooperating with police, darlings. Everyone.

**Hook:** Her phone still shows a livestream scheduled for tomorrow. Its title: "The Big Announcement."

**Effects:** Skyline Pier Residences: lobby and sidewalk only through Day 8. Story task: leave flowers at the pier. Chatter phase: early.

### Beat 2 (Day 2): "Vigil, Vibes, and a Very Missing Boyfriend"

*Previously:* Sarah Bird died after a Sunday brunch with her mom and her pastor.

Hundreds packed Hunter's Pier for a candlelight vigil led by Pastor Vale, co-trustee of Sarah's charity, the Bird Song Foundation. Loretta livestreamed it with a ring light "for the fans." Boyfriend Jett Calloway didn't show, saying he was "at the gym all afternoon" on Sunday.

**Hook:** Pressed Iron Gym says Jett cancelled his membership two months ago.

**Effects:** Free story event: candlelight vigil at Hunter's Pier (attend for clout). Story task: light a candle at the vigil.

### Beat 3 (Day 3): "Best Friend, Better Secret"

*Previously:* Sarah's boyfriend Jett skipped her vigil, and his gym alibi doesn't exist.

Photos surfaced of Sarah holding hands in a Rockaway Diner booth with Theo Marsh: Jett's best friend and the co-writer of "Glass Bird." A source says she ended it the night before she died, and Theo replied, "You'll regret this."

**Hook:** A service-door camera caught Theo entering her building at 2:15 PM on Sunday.

**Effects:** Prediction P1 opens. Rockaway Diner gets a "Sarah's booth" tag, and visit prices rise 50% through Day 6 as fans pile in. Story task: share the rumor.

### Beat 4 (Day 4): "The Doorman's Ledger"

*Previously:* Sarah was secretly seeing Theo, Jett's best friend, who was at her building the day she died.

Doorman Ray keeps a paper sign-out book, and police have it. Loretta left at 12:10 PM and "slammed the lobby door hard enough to wake Brooklyn." Pastor Vale signed out at 12:52 PM, though he told reporters he left "right behind Loretta."

**Hook:** One more entry: Jett's key fob opened the garage at 1:34 PM. So much for the gym.

**Effects:** P1 closes at end of day. Story task: ask Ray what he saw (visit the Skyline Pier lobby). Chatter phase: mid.

### Beat 5 (Day 5): "Flowers at the Door"

*Previously:* the doorman's book puts Loretta, the pastor and Jett at Sarah's building on Sunday.

Police confirm Theo left white roses and a note at Sarah's door: "I'm sorry. I'll wait as long as you need. T." He says he knocked at 2:15 PM and nobody answered. Fans also found Sarah's last story, posted 12:31 PM: a teacup, a sun-logo thermos, and the caption "one last cup before the big news."

**Hook:** If Sarah couldn't answer the door at 2:15, what happened between 12:31 and 2:15?

**Effects:** P1 revealed (Theo came to apologize). Story task: rewatch Sarah's last story (evidence card).

### Beat 6 (Day 6): "Momager Math"

*Previously:* Sarah stopped answering her door sometime between 12:31 PM and 2:15 PM on Sunday.

A leaked contract shows Loretta earns 25% of everything Sarah makes, forever, and is beneficiary of a $3M life policy. Sarah had booked an entertainment lawyer for Monday to "end a management agreement." Loretta also dropped a "Forever Our Bird" hoodie, $85, "for healing."

**Hook:** A police inventory leak lists sleeping pills in Loretta's handbag.

**Effects:** P2 (whodunit) opens. "Forever Our Bird" hoodie appears in the Shop (Fashion) at $85 in-game.

### Beat 7 (Day 7): "The Tea, the Thermos, and the Gift"

*Previously:* Loretta stood to lose a fortune if Sarah fired her, and police found sleeping pills in her bag.

Toxicology: Sarah had a sedative in her system, carried in her tea, and it is not the drug Loretta is prescribed. The sun-logo thermos from her last story is nowhere in the apartment. Sarah's accountant says she asked for a full audit of the Bird Song Foundation on the Friday before she died, and Pastor Vale just announced the Harbor Dome groundbreaking, "thanks to an anonymous gift."

**Hook:** Jett's lawyer, unprompted: "My client took nothing from that apartment except what was his."

**Effects:** P3 (thermos) opens. **P2 and P3 close at end of day.** Harbor Dome groundbreaking gala announced at Radiant Harbor Church. Story task: visit Radiant Harbor Church.

### Beat 8 (Day 8): "Case Closed? (Queens Thinks So)"

*Previously:* the sedative was in Sarah's tea, the thermos is missing, and Jett's lawyer is nervous.

Jett was brought in for questioning after bank records showed $40,000 moved from Sarah's account to his resale business. Loretta posted "JUSTICE" in giant letters, and Pastor Vale prayed for Jett on *Blessed & Booked*. The comment section has closed the case.

**Hook:** Detective Nia Okafor skipped the press conference. She was spotted in Astoria, outside a church, holding a warrant and a photo of a thermos.

**Effects:** Biggest cliffhanger. The card shows "Voting closed. The truth drops tomorrow" with the Day 9 date. Chatter phase: late.

### Beat 9 (Day 9): "The Pastor Is Not Available for Comment"

*Previously:* the internet blamed Jett, but the detective went to Astoria.

Pastor Gideon Vale was arrested at Radiant Harbor Church, where police found the sun-logo thermos washed and back on a shelf. The audit Sarah ordered shows $1.2M moved from her foundation to a company building the Harbor Dome. Elevator logs show Jett never left the garage level, and Vale was alone with Sarah for 42 minutes.

**Hook:** Police also recovered Sarah's draft for "The Big Announcement." Tomorrow, Queens finally hears it.

**Effects:** P2 and P3 revealed; "Called It" and "Lola's Favorite" badges awarded. Harbor Dome gala cancelled. Radiant Harbor Church closed (police activity). Skyline Pier restrictions lift.

### Beat 10 (Day 10): "The Big Announcement"

*Previously:* Pastor Vale was arrested for Sarah's death after police found the thermos at his church.

Sarah's draft was simple: she was leaving the church, handing her foundation to independent trustees, releasing her own album, and "starting over with only the people who never asked me for anything." Theo gets his songwriting credit, Jett has to repay the $40K, and Loretta quietly pulled the hoodie. Rest easy, Glass Bird.

**Hook:** Lola's sign-off: "Ray the doorman says someone new just moved into the penthouse. And Loretta is already asking for their number."

**Effects:** Hoodie removed from the Shop (buyers keep theirs). Skyline Pier becomes a memorial wall (visit and leave flowers for small clout). Chatter phase: post. Teases the next arc (recurring cast: Loretta, Ray, Okafor).

## Premium side arcs

Three side arcs at $2.99 each (Stripe Checkout, no clout), 5 beats each. Side beats drop on Days 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9, so the side arcs move with the case. Every side arc makes one loud suspect look worse. None contains the answer, and nothing released before voting closes points at the pastor or the church. Each final beat only lands after the reveal.

**Free player view:** title, teaser line, and a locked card per released beat (headline only).

### Side arc A (perspective): "Jett: The Plus-One"

*Teaser (free):* "He was in every photo. He was never in the caption."

Focus: the relationship from Jett's side, and the stolen $40K.

- **Day 1, "Always the Accessory."** Jett on life as the blur behind her selfies, and Loretta's nickname for him: "the accessory." Surprise: he'd bought an engagement ring. Hook: he paid for it with Sarah's card.
- **Day 3, "I Knew About Theo."** Jett knew about the affair for three weeks and said nothing, because Theo had lent him $15K. Hook: his unsent Saturday-night text to Theo: "After tomorrow we settle everything."
- **Day 5, "The $40K."** Jett calls the transfers "loans" for a sneaker drop. Sarah found out the Thursday before she died and told him, "I'm not mad. I'm done." Hook: she let him keep the money, so why did he go to her garage on Sunday?
- **Day 7, "Twenty Minutes in the Garage."** He went to grab $40K of sneakers from his car before she could claim them, then sat in the garage for twenty minutes deciding whether to go up and propose anyway. Hook: his phone rings. It's Detective Okafor.
- **Day 9, "The Speech I Didn't Give" (post-reveal).** His proposal speech, read aloud. He admits he loved the life more than he loved her. Surprise: the proposal was Loretta's idea. Hook: he gives Loretta the ring to return, and she doesn't.

*Worth-paying check:* new information (the ring, the $15K debt, the garage), real surprises, its own hooks, and it makes Jett look guiltier without solving anything.

### Side arc B (evidence): "The Momager Files"

*Teaser (free):* "Loretta kept every receipt. Every single one."

Focus: Loretta's sense that she is owed, told through documents.

- **Day 1, "Contract, Page 14."** The management deal: 25% forever, including Sarah's "likeness after death." Surprise: Loretta drafted it herself on Sarah's 16th birthday. Hook: a sticky note on page 14: "She'll never read this far."

- **Day 5, "The Policy."** The $3M life policy, with premiums paid from Sarah's account, raised by Loretta this summer. Surprise: last month Sarah filled in a beneficiary change form. Hook: she never signed it.
- **Day 7, "The Receipts Folder."** "What I'm owed" was $60K Loretta spent on Sarah's first demo and first lease when Sarah was 17, and she kept every receipt. Sarah's statements also show $30K a month to Loretta and Jett's "loans." Hook: the last receipt is a florist order for white roses, placed Sunday at 11:02 AM.
- **Day 9, "What Loretta Kept" (post-reveal).** The roses were a brunch peace offering with a card, "I'm sorry too. Mom," never delivered because she stormed out. Surprise: Sarah had drafted a reply promising her mother 10% for life. One more line in Sarah's statements: a $25K monthly "tithe" to Radiant Harbor, which stopped the day she ordered the audit. Hook: Loretta reads the draft and asks if it's legally binding.

*Worth-paying check:* the strongest misdirection in the package (the 11:02 AM roses) is resolved inside the arc. The tithe now appears only after the reveal, so paid players get no edge on P2 or P3.

### Side arc C (insider): "Ray's Night Book"

*Teaser (free):* "Twenty years at the front desk. One tenant asked him to write everything down."
